How each one stays private

BTC

Sent as a silent payment (BIP‑352): each payment lands at a fresh address that only the recipient’s key can find, so nothing on chain ties it to their published address.

TAC

Paid inside the shielded pool on Bitcoin: amounts and parties are hidden behind a zero-knowledge proof made on this device; the relay posts it and takes its fee in TAC.

ETH

Paid inside the private pool on Ethereum, Base or Robinhood Chain, proved on this device and sent by a relay, so no gas and no link between payer and payee.
